# Residential Lease Agreement

This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is made on 1 June 2025 between Harbor Point Properties LLC (the "Landlord") and Maria Gomez (the "Tenant") for the premises at 418 Willow Street, Apartment 3B, Portland, Oregon 97209 (the "Premises").

## 1. Term

The Lease begins on 1 July 2025 and ends on 30 June 2026. It is a fixed term of twelve months. If the Tenant remains in possession after 30 June 2026 with the Landlord's consent and no new lease is signed, the tenancy converts to month-to-month on the same terms, and either party may end it with thirty days' written notice.

## 2. Rent

Monthly rent is $1,850.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent is paid by bank transfer to the account named in Schedule A or through the Harbor Point tenant portal. Rent received after the fifth day of the month incurs a late fee of $75.00. A returned payment incurs a $35.00 charge in addition to any late fee.

## 3. Security deposit

The Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,000.00, held in a separate account at Cascade Community Bank. The deposit will be returned within thirty-one days after the Tenant moves out, less any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear. An itemized statement of deductions will accompany any partial refund.

## 4. Utilities

The Landlord pays for water, sewer, and trash collection. The Tenant is responsible for electricity, natural gas, and internet service and must keep electricity and gas connected in the Tenant's name for the full term.

## 5. Occupants and guests

The Premises may be occupied only by the Tenant and one additional adult occupant, Luis Gomez. Guests may stay no more than fourteen consecutive nights without the Landlord's written consent.

## 6. Pets

One cat is permitted under this Lease. The Tenant has paid a one-time non-refundable pet fee of $300.00. Dogs, reptiles, and any additional animals require prior written approval and may be subject to an additional fee. The Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the cat.

## 7. Parking

One assigned parking space, number 14 in the rear lot, is included in the rent. A second space may be rented for $90.00 per month if available.

## 8. Maintenance and repairs

The Landlord will keep the structure, plumbing, heating, and appliances supplied with the Premises in good working order. The Tenant must report needed repairs through the tenant portal or by calling (503) 555-0188. Non-emergency repairs will be attended to within seven days. The Tenant may not make alterations, paint, or install fixtures without written permission.

## 9. Entry

Except in an emergency, the Landlord will give at least twenty-four hours' notice before entering the Premises for inspection, repairs, or showings, and will enter only between 9 AM and 7 PM.

## 10. Renewal and termination

The Landlord will offer renewal terms no later than 1 May 2026. If the Tenant wishes to move out at the end of the term, the Tenant must give written notice at least thirty days before 30 June 2026. Early termination requires sixty days' notice and a fee equal to one month's rent, unless the Tenant is relocating for active military duty.

## 11. Insurance

The Tenant must maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000 in liability coverage for the full term and provide proof of coverage before move-in.
